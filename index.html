About MJ

Milan Joshi (मिलन जोशी) also known as Milan J., is a dedicated Life Insurance Advisor and former Project Coordinator based in Kathmandu, Nepal. He brings a rare blend of strategic thinking, operational expertise, and organizational insight, helping businesses and individuals navigate complex challenges with clarity and precision.

With experience spanning project management, life insurance consulting, administration, and corporate social responsibility, Milan combines analytical rigor with a people-first approach to deliver meaningful results.

His work focuses on building efficient systems, fostering team growth, and creating long-term value for clients and communities alike.

Vision Statement
To empower individuals and organizations in Nepal to achieve financial security, operational excellence, and lasting positive impact through ethical advisory, innovative project management, and community-focused initiatives.

Mission Statement
To deliver reliable life insurance solutions while leveraging project coordination and management expertise to enhance systems, support local communities, and create measurable, sustainable outcomes.

Over the years, he has worked across diverse teams and initiatives, building operational frameworks, improving coordination, and supporting organizations in achieving sustainable outcomes. He values ethical practice, transparent communication, and consistency—because shortcuts look good today and fail tomorrow.

At his core, Milan is driven by long-term value creation. He is committed to helping individuals secure their financial future, assisting organizations in strengthening their internal systems, and contributing to community-level progress in Nepal through responsible, people-centric work.

Furthore more, Milan J. is also passionate about the Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) 🌈 —a set of 17 interconnected global goals established by the United Nations (UN) in 2015.

These goals aim to address some of the world's most pressing challenges and promote sustainable development across economic, social, and environmental dimensions.



In a fast-moving world, the Sustainable Development Goals are our compass, reminding us that progress means nothing if people or the planet are left behind.

They call us to act with purpose—driving sustainable development in Nepal by empowering communities, protecting the environment, and ensuring growth and equality move together. When ambition turns into action, lasting impact follows.
